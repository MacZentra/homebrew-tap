## Short description

A native Mac disk analyzer and storage cleaner for Apple silicon. It scans locally, shows what is using space, and finds caches, large files, duplicates and app leftovers. Removed files go to the Trash first, and system files are protected.

## Facts worth quoting

- The scan covers a 256 GB disk in about 15 to 20 seconds on Apple silicon.
- Cleaning moves items to the Trash first, so nothing is deleted permanently by the app.
- Developer junk it finds: Xcode DerivedData, `node_modules`, Docker.raw, Conda and Python environments.
- A `msc` command-line companion (Pro) runs the same scans from Terminal.
